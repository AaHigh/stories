Spidey Senses and Murder

The city manager reached for a comic book. The councilman reached for a homicide charge. A resident reached for the fallacy. The street did not move.

Published August 28, 2026 · Tower District, Fresno

Language

Unhoused. Spidey senses. Murder.

Three words from the dais that do work. Soften the sidewalk. Inflate the federal agent. Hide the local failure.

Office

Nonpartisan on the ballot

Miguel Arias, District 3, terms out January 2027. Now running for Fresno Unified Trustee Area 1 on November 3, 2026.

Street

Vacancy, vice, inherited gang culture

Tower still has buildings that have been dead for decades. The vacancy pilot produced almost no teeth. Optics love a crisis.

City Hall has a talent for renaming the thing in front of your face.

People sleeping in doorways become the unhoused. A manager’s hunch becomes Spidey senses. A contested federal shooting in Minnesota, still without a completed local homicide verdict against the agent, becomes murder from a Fresno microphone. Then the camera pans away from Olive, from Fulton after five, from the boarded shops, from the set that actually runs the block.

That swap is not manners. It is policy. It teaches the room who is allowed to be the villain.

On January 15, 2026, Councilmember Miguel Arias used the word killed for the Minneapolis ICE shooting of Renee Good. Two weeks later, on January 29, he escalated. On the official stream he asked whether Fresno police would arrest an ICE agent “if an ICE agent commits murder as they have in Minnesota.” He also spoke of “the murder of Alex.” GV Wire logged the shift from killed to murder. His own District 3 page posted the comments.

The city manager’s register is different and just as useful. On the January 29 stream at 1:01:03 she talks about break-ins by unhoused people. Comic radar. Soft noun. Hard crime. The fallacy is the same in both mouths: treat language as proof, treat the federal jacket as the local emergency, and treat the district’s wreckage as weather.

A resident stood up and named it. Equivocation. Loaded word. Motte and bailey. Call it what you want. The street is still the street.

What the words do

Unhoused is a hygiene word. It rinses behavior off the noun. On Tape 5 the city manager does not say burglars. She says a site had been broken into a number of times by unhoused people. The act is a break-in. The label is a status. That is the loaded label working in the other direction from murder. Murder inflates a federal shooting into a closed case. Unhoused shrinks a crime into a social condition.

At what point does the crime get serious enough that the person is a criminal? Do they have to murder someone first? Or is the quiet rule that Fresno will not spend the money to find people on misdemeanors if those people are unhoused, because that enforcement became unpopular, and because the government learned that unpopularity is cheaper than a conviction? Soft language is how public safety steps off the hook. It does not repeal the Penal Code. It just refuses to say the word that would require using it.

Spidey senses is the cute twin of the same dodge. A city manager is not Peter Parker. The job is counts, beds, warrants, contracts, and whether anyone finishes treatment. Cute radar is how a government admits it is guessing while sounding like it has a gift.

Murder, used that way, is a verdict. Listen for that word on Tape 1. GV Wire recorded the shift from killed to murder, then sold a calmer civic portrait than the microphone earned. That is how a loaded label survives a news cycle. Minnesota’s cases are ugly and unfinished. Federal officials called self-defense. Minneapolis officials called a killing. An ICE officer in a separate January shooting was later charged by a Minnesota county. Ross, in the Good case, had not been criminally charged as of mid-2026. A Fresno councilmember does not close that file by raising the noun. He uses the noun to instruct the local audience: the federal government is the predator, your city should treat the agent as a suspect, and cooperation is contamination.

That is the cultural norm. Government as the bad guy. The coalition as the wounded. The block as scenery.

How the policy lands in District 3

Arias did not invent homelessness. He did spend years as the district’s loudest interpreter of it. He opposed some housing moves by citing neighbors left out of the conversation. He helped pass and later amend the camping ordinance. He warned the public about ICE at Cherry Auction and about a Fresno-Bakersfield surge that did not arrive as billed. Vendors asked for his resignation. He said he would keep warning. He told the room California is a sanctuary state and SB 54 is the rule.

Meanwhile the district kept looking like a training map. Vacant commercial buildings. A Tower vacancy pilot that, after a year, had almost nothing to show but one stubborn fine. Sales-tax rebate theater. Night streets with very little legal commerce in front of your face. Inherited gang culture on the same ground, the Fresno Bulldogs, a primarily Mexican-American independent set with a documented file of drugs, guns, intimidation, and cruelty cases. That is not his ethnicity. That is the set. Pretending the two are the same is sloppy. Pretending the set is a rumor is also sloppy.

Money moved. Outcomes lagged. The city spent millions on shelters and still could not say who finished Treatment First. It sued over a stalled tiny-home deal that had already burned public funds. It lost Prohousing status in 2025. It announced progress with a new way of counting. Dyer said zero encampments if you define an encampment as ten people for ten days. The canal bank does not care about the definition.

Arias’s particular harm is the inversion. He takes a nonpartisan seat and runs a national morality play. ICE murder. Raid alerts. Pledge posture. Then the local file (vacancy, vice, grants, gang pipeline) becomes someone else’s hate. That is not representation. That is cover.

He is not a convicted racketeer. Do not write that lie. Civic corruption here is the cheaper kind. Narrative patronage. Time on the dais spent teaching the district to fear the wrong badge. A politics of vice that prefers a permanent emergency because a working block would not need him as translator.

Nick Shirley and the new accountability clock

In late December 2025 a YouTuber named Nick Shirley walked up to Minnesota day cares that were drawing public child-care money and asked a rude question: where are the children? The video was messy, ideological, and incomplete. It was also a camera. State inspectors scrambled. Federal agents later hit about twenty centers. In July 2026, Fahima Mahamud, whose Future Leaders site was in that video, pleaded guilty to wire fraud and conspiracy. Prosecutors said more than $4.6 million in false Child Care Assistance claims, plus earlier Feeding Our Future money. Feeding Our Future was already a $250 million case with dozens of convictions. The Minnesota surge that Arias turned into a murder sermon sat on top of that fraud story whether he liked the sequencing or not.

The lesson is not that every viral clip is a brief. The lesson is that the era of unfilmed systems is over. Empty buildings that bill the treasury get walked. Dais language gets clipped. Grant pipelines get named. People who used to hide inside compassion vocabulary now meet a record.

Fresno is not Minneapolis. Do not import a Somali daycare case onto a District 3 councilman and call it justice. Import the method. Official tape. Contracts. Point-in-time definitions. Shelter invoices. Vacancy citations. Who got the ARPA. Who finished treatment. Who warned of a raid that did not happen. Who called a shooting murder from a chair that cannot issue an indictment.

How he can actually be held to account

He is terming out of District 3. The council seat is already a 2026 race. That door is closing. The live door is the Fresno Unified Trustee Area 1 ballot on November 3, 2026. School boards are where compassion language goes to reproduce. Voters can decide whether a man who used a city dais to try federal homicide cases is the person they want near children, budgets, and discipline.

Other lawful pressure, none of it theatrical:

  • Keep every official stream clipped and timestamped. This page is that work.
  • Public records on raid warnings, HEART team outcomes, shelter contracts, and the tiny-home lawsuit.
  • Demand written definitions. Encampment. Unhoused. Completed treatment. Vacant.
  • Ethics and FPPC filings if the issue is money and disclosure. Not vibes.
  • Do not invent a crime to win an argument. The Minnesota fraud cases moved because prosecutors had claims and bank records. Fresno’s file is civic. Treat it as civic until it is not.

August 19 was the other half of the same sentence. Grow southeast if you want. If the core stays a mission map, you are not building a city. You are annexing an alibi.

Close

If the government is always the bad guy, nobody has to fix the block. That is the racket. Spidey senses are not a plan. Murder is not a memo. District 3 is a real place with real vacancies, real vice, and a real gang culture that does not care what you call it. The tapes are public. The next ballot is marked. Use both.
