Elevated by design

High TOWER Pizza is a ghost kitchen concept rooted in the Tower District. Real dough. Real sauce. Real cheese. Real heat. No corporate chain, no delivery app tax, no fluorescent box. Just a home kitchen that knows the neighborhood and an electric cargo bike that keeps the pie hot and the carbon low.

The name is deliberate. High. Tower. The same neon landmark that has watched over this corner of Fresno for decades. We lean into the elevated vibe — chill, friendly, a little green around the edges — without spelling everything out. You already know what that means.

From the kitchen

Hand-built in a residential ghost kitchen. Sauce down, cheese on, into the Whirlpool, out to the street.

One slice at a time

No sixteen-inch industrial disc. Just honest portions that travel well in the cargo box and still taste like they left the oven five minutes ago. The kind of pie you eat standing in the kitchen, then immediately want another.

Cheese-forward. Sauce that actually tastes like tomatoes. Crust with a little chew and a little char.

The vehicle

Electric Bakfiets-style cargo bike. Mid-drive power. Room for multiple pies, sides, and the occasional green special. Quiet enough for the neighborhood after dark.

Cargo capacity + range

  • → Large front box holds multiple pizzas + sides upright and hot
  • → 1250W mid-drive, 6-speed, ~40 mile range
  • → LCD display, lights, and real brakes for Fresno streets
  • → Zero exhaust. Low noise. High elevation.

The same class of bike used by families and small businesses that need to move volume without a van.

The friendly high

We don’t put a leaf on the logo. We don’t need to. The name does the work. High TOWER. Elevated delivery. Green light specials when the kitchen is feeling generous.

This is Tower District energy: a little late, a little loud, a little greener than the strip-mall chains. Come for the pizza. Stay for the vibe. Or just text the number and watch the cargo bike roll up under the neon.
