A Party Does Not Clear the Sidewalk

The Tower Neighborhood Association is raising money and throwing FernFest to fill vacant spaces. That is not the same work as stopping open-air drug use between Deli Delicious and Cuca's.

Place

Dollar Tree / Tower Market lot. Fern and Maroa if you prefer the nicer name. Southeast corner of Broadway and Olive after that.

What was posted

Friends of Tower PorchFest pointed neighbors to vibrantnotvacant.info and a party on October 24.

What was seen

Open use of a smokable drug, foil with black marks, a blanket over a head, a shopping cart moving west on the sidewalk.

Today two people used drugs in the open between Deli Delicious and Cuca's Mexican restaurant. One of them goes by Suzanne. She is new to the area. The other person is new too.

A few minutes later Suzanne was on the sidewalk at the southeast corner of Broadway and Olive, westbound, with a shopping cart. She held tinfoil with black marks. She looked disoriented. Just before that she had been crouched facing a wall. The person with her had a head under a blanket in the posture you use when you do not want a glass pipe seen.

This was the first time I have seen her obviously under the influence of something smokable and harder than marijuana. I have spoken with her more than once. She has been around two or three weeks.

The campaign and the sidewalk

The Tower Neighborhood Association, fiscal sponsor of Tower Porchfest, is running a campaign to highlight and fill vacant spaces. They are throwing FernFest on October 24. There are other ways to get involved. The link is clean. The language is clean. Nobody in that post has to say the word pipe.

Vacant buildings in this neighborhood are real. Cafe Midi sat empty for decades. Chicken Pie Shop on Olive has been dark since 2019. The old Brass Unicorn storefront on Fern was photographed boarded in 2026 next to other closed rooms. The city ran a vacant commercial building pilot. After about a year, public reporting said one documented citation. That is the official story of vacancy.

Vacancy is not why a new woman is bent over a wall with foil in her hand at Broadway and Olive. Vacancy is what you talk about when you do not want to talk about Health and Safety Code.

Why shops leave

Ask the people who ran The Brass Unicorn why the shop is not still in that Fern block. The short version neighbors already know: a pattern of street crime that did not get written up the way it would get written up in a zip code with better optics.

Police under this political class will occasionally produce a felony when the picture is perfect. Dogs with drugs inside them made the news because the picture was perfect. Daily open use in the Dollar Tree and Tower Market parking field does not make the news because it is ordinary. Ordinary is the point. Ordinary is what a tenant prices when they decide whether to sign a lease.

Nobody wants to eat next to it. Nobody wants to sell books next to it. Nobody wants to let a child walk past it. That is not a branding problem. That is an enforcement problem dressed up as a vacancy problem so the city can keep chasing grant language.

Guilt by standing nearby

If you live out here you already know the social tax. Talk to the person on the cart and someone will call you a tweaker. A man who is not playing with a full deck, and who is very good on saxophone, has already used that word on me. That is why a lot of people will not deal with the problem. They do not want the smear. Critical thinking is not the neighborhood's strongest product.

Suzanne is a young woman destroying herself in public. People will say she is cute and then look away. I have seen her working her own interests with a man on a motorcycle in front of Tower Sports Bar. Older men take an interest. That arrangement has a name in every city. It is not a vacant-storefront ordinance.

What the party cannot do

FernFest can put music on porches. Porchfest already does that well. Filling a dark room with a new tenant is a good project if the sidewalk in front of the room is lawful. If the sidewalk is an open market for foil and glass, the tenant is being asked to subsidize the city's refusal to write tickets.

The derivative problem is simple. People will not linger where open-air use is tolerated. Businesses will not stay where open-air use is tolerated. Campaigns that skip that sentence are not campaigns. They are cover.

Until officials admit they will not prosecute the cases that produce felonies because the politics and the federal money run the other direction, the same block will look the same after October 24. The lights on the stage will come down. The cart will still be on Olive.

A neighborhood association can fill a room. It cannot fill a sidewalk the city has decided not to police. Say the second sentence out loud or the first one is advertising.
