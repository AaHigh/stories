The Same Standard Applied — Single-Bungee JBL Charge Mount

Commercial mounts for a JBL Charge on BMX or plus bars range from $15 multi-strap kits to $130 CNC aluminum clamps. Most still allow vibration loosening, button interference, or added mass. The single high-quality bungee cord method developed here produces zero slippage and zero functional problems under actual Tower District riding conditions. Cost is a few dollars. Mass penalty is near zero. Geometry creates continuous radial compression and axial lock simultaneously.

This is operations research applied to a daily tool: the winning cost-benefit formula for the exact use case. It was developed the same way the 2019 hubs entry was developed — observe the real problem, refuse the mediocre answers, iterate until the solution is functionally unbeatable.
