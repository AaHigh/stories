To anyone in politics

Whistleblow or Go Down

Blow the whistle. Or take the ride to a federal penitentiary. Public safety does not survive a silent dais.

This is not a homeless problem. That word is a loaded label. It rinses the crime off the pile.

This is a criminal piece of $#!+ problem. Unused law. Federal money treated like a community mascot. People in office who will not say the statute out loud.

If you hold a seat, staff a seat, bid a contract, or sit in a lobby and call yourself a journalist, you already know enough to pick a side. Become a whistleblower. Or go down with the people who thought silence was a career plan.

The tape

Why the penitentiary is not a metaphor

Minnesota already ran the experiment. Child-nutrition money. Fake sites. Federal prison. Aimee Bock, 500 months. People walked back from overseas to face the docket.

The same class of money moves here. Child-serving. Housing. Arts. Emergency. The invoices look local. The source is often federal. When the audit arrives, "I was just doing services" is not a defense. "I did not want to hurt my friends" is not a defense. "We used a softer noun" is not a defense.

California still has Penal Code 374.7. Dump in a canal and it is a misdemeanor with a mandatory fine. The laterals behind this district are not mysterious. Unused law is a signal. Predators read signals.

California still has Penal Code 311.11. A registrant still walked into a Fresno council race because the state left the door open. That is not compassion. That is a captured gate.

The employment offer

This message is for anybody involved in politics in this city, this county, this state.

If you have seen the billing, the unused statute, the loaded label, the kid-money, the lobby act: document it. Take it to a prosecutor, an inspector general, a federal agent, a paper that will print the invoice. Do it while you can still walk in as a witness.

If you wait until the indictment list is public, you are not a reformer. You are a passenger.

I dropped a District 3 ticket for this reason. I will not warranty a cracked part with my name. The outside is the job. Expose them. Spare the public. Spare yourself the ride.

The line

Whistleblow. Or go down.

Public safety is the protected class. Not the brand. Not the noun. Not the lobby.
